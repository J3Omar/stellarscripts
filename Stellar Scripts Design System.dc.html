Stellar Scripts — Design System
Stellar Scripts
Design System · v1 · NASA Space Apps 2026

A mission archive kept in a study at the edge of space. Warm paper, leather and gilt carry memory; star fields, Mars prints and cool cosmic light carry the journey. Muted teal appears only where something is alive and can be touched.

01 · ARCHIVE
Warm is memory
Amber, cream and leather carry every surface. Nothing is pure white or pure black.
02 · COSMOS
Cool is distance
Nebula rose and deep indigo glow at the edges of the room, never on the page.
03 · LIVE
Teal is alive
Muted teal is reserved for hover, focus, playback and anything you can press.
